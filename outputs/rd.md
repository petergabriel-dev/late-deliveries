# Donut regression discontinuity at the promised date

Generated by `make rd`. **Corroborating estimate, not the headline** (ADR-0007 §3-4). Outcome: share of 1-2 star reviews (`low_review`, 0-1 scale) on headline orders with a review. Running variable: days late; cutoff 0.5 (day +1 and later are late); local-linear fit, triangular kernel, fixed bandwidth h (days with |days_late - cutoff| < h); rdrobust with mass-point adjustment and SEs clustered by seller (CR1, rdrobust's `vce='cr1'`). Estimate of record: conventional. 95% CI: robust bias-corrected, which is centered on the bias-corrected estimate (shown for reference), so the conventional estimate can fall outside it. Specification: ADR-0008 §3.

Days columns: range of days late used on each side (number of distinct days).

Caveats: the estimate is local to orders near the promised date, while the money translation needs the average over all late orders (median 7 days late). From day 3 late on, most reviews are written before the order arrives (survey timing), so the right side mixes in the experience of waiting. Orders bunch just before the promised date (Phase 1 diagnostics), which is why the donut drops days 0 and +1.
