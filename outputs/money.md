# Phase 4 money translation: what one late delivery costs

Generated by `make money` from `orders.parquet`, `fe.json` and `assumptions.toml`. Every formula, input and flag rule was fixed in ADR-0013 before this run; flags are computed by code. Figures are per late headline order (6,518 late orders, 6,366 with a review), as platform revenue (lost GMV x take rate). USD at R$ 3.3305 per US$ (mean of 20 monthly BCB PTAX averages, 2017-01 to 2018-08). The range is bounds (every input at its low-cost or high-cost end), not a confidence interval.

## Specification echo

- unit: per late headline order; BRL, USD = BRL / mean monthly BCB PTAX rate
- platform_revenue: lost GMV (item price) x take rate, channels 1-2; channel 3 is a direct cost
- channel2_anchor: review_answer_ts of the late order; later orders in (answer, answer + H]
- eligible: answer + H < 2018-09-01
- later_order_excluded_statuses: ['canceled', 'unavailable']
- horizons_days: [30, 90, 180]
- range: every input at its low-cost end to every input at its high-cost end: bounds, not a confidence interval
- measured_share_min: 0.1
- window_months: 20
