Read with care: the estimates swing with bandwidth and polynomial order (sign and significance change across rows), because the count of orders falls sharply at days -6/-5, inside the wider windows. The automatic bandwidth returns an invalid negative density. The raw counts are the steadier evidence: orders fall 36% from day 0 to day +1, versus an average fall of 10% per day over the five days before.
